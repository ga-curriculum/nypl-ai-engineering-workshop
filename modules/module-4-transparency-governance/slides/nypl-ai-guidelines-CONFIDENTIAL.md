## NYPL's Approach to AI

As a trusted steward of knowledge dedicated to equitable access to information, NYPL has a responsibility to meet the challenges of AI thoughtfully. Per their five-year strategy, the Library's approach is designed to understand, adopt, and help shape this emerging technology ethically.

## Core Objectives

1. **Empower Through Education** — Foster AI literacy among staff and patrons to close the digital divide
2. **Enhance, Not Replace** — AI enables staff to focus on the deeply human aspects of the Library's mission
3. **Advance Library-Centric Solutions** — Harness AI to upgrade digital infrastructure, unlock collection potential, and improve patron services

## Guiding Principles

| Principle | Description |
|---|---|
| **Human-First Approach** | AI is a tool, not a substitute for human judgment or creativity. AI systems should be developed with a human-centered approach. |
| **Accountability** | Oversight is essential for accuracy, ethical decision-making, and legal compliance. An individual or group always remains responsible for AI-generated work. |
| **Equity** | AI-powered systems must increase access, not create barriers. Wider accessibility considerations must be included. |
| **Security** | AI systems must uphold NYPL's security standards and ensure data is protected from unauthorized access, breaches, or loss. |
| **Privacy** | Safeguard confidential information, PII, and other sensitive data from unauthorized access, disclosure, and manipulation. |
| **Transparency** | Be transparent about when, where, and how AI is used. Prioritize AI systems that share this value. |
| **Accuracy** | AI-generated content is held to the same standards as human-created content. Information integrity is paramount. |
| **Ethical Conduct** | Never use AI to fabricate, distort, or exploit a person's behavior, identity, or vulnerabilities (age, disability, socioeconomic status, etc.). |
| **Digital Literacy** | Promote understanding of how AI works, including the debates concerning opportunities and risks. |
| **Protecting IP Rights** | AI must not violate intellectual property rights, rights of publicity, or other proprietary rights. |
| **Sustainability** | Use AI thoughtfully and purposefully. Consider non-AI approaches when equally effective, given AI's environmental impact. |

## Working Staff Guidelines

### What To Do

- **Use Only Pre-Approved AI Systems** — Confirm the tool is vetted by IT/Digital. Public versions may not offer the same protections as enterprise versions. Use the approved version logged in with Library credentials.
- **Review, Revise, and Fact-Check** — You are responsible for all material created with AI. All AI-generated material must meet the same standards as human-created work. Fact-check using reliable, authoritative, current sources.
- **Treat AI Outputs as Library Business** — Work outputs using AI are held to the same standards as any other Library work product (per Employee Handbook).
- **Label AI-Generated Work** — If AI fully generated or significantly modified a permitted work output (charts, diagrams, infographics, transcriptions, translations), clearly label it as AI-generated.
- **Give Notice / Obtain Consent** — Before using AI with audio or video notetaking/recording abilities, notify participants and gain consent.
- **Stay Updated on These Guidelines** — Guidance will change with technology, laws, and best practices.

### What NOT To Do

- **Do Not Use Unapproved AI Systems for Library Business** — Data entered into unapproved systems could be exposed (training data, breaches). Pre-approved tools are vetted for security, compliance, and privacy.
- **Do Not Violate Library Policies or the Law** — Do not use AI in ways that violate laws, NYPL's Privacy Policy, Employee Handbook, or any other Library policy.
- **Do Not Deceive** — Do not use AI with the intent or effect of deception or manipulation. No fabricated images, deepfakes, or misinformation.
- **Do Not Use AI for Public-Facing Images, Video or Audio** — All AI-generated images (including illustrations), video or audio are prohibited for public-facing work, with the exception of charts and infographics.
- **Do Not Use AI for HR/Employment Decisions** — Employment decisions (promotions, advancement) are the responsibility of the supervisor or hiring manager.
- **Do Not Use AI for Work Under Another Department's Oversight** — Route requests through appropriate teams and channels.
- **Do Not Generate Marketing Materials or Modify Brand Assets** — Do not use generative AI to produce marketing materials or manipulate trademarked brand assets (e.g., logo). Visit the NYPL Brand Hub for guidance.

## Key Definitions (from NYPL document)

- **AI System:** Any data system, software, hardware, application, tool, or utility that operates in whole or in part using AI.
- **Generative AI:** A subset of AI that produces text, images, videos, audio, or other forms of data.
- **External Use of AI:** When AI outputs directly interact with or impact non-Library stakeholders.
- **Internal Use of AI:** When AI impacts are confined to the Library's internal operations.
- **PII:** Information that directly identifies an individual or can be used to indirectly identify them.
- **Work Outputs:** Concrete products or results produced for official library business, internally and externally.

## Roles & Responsibilities

- **CDO + VPIT** — Accountable leaders for AI implementation and oversight; jointly responsible for updating these guidelines.
- **AI Working Group Leads** — Ensure AI systems comply with Information and Security Policy.
- **Director of Privacy and Compliance** — Oversees digital privacy practices and responsible technology usage.
- **HR / Learning and Development** — Ensures staff receive necessary training to use AI ethically, safely, and effectively.
- **General Counsel** — Advises on legal issues or risks associated with AI usage.
- **All Library departments** — Responsible for following and regularly reviewing these guidelines.

## Applicability & Compliance

- Guidelines apply to all staff (including consultants, contractors, partners, volunteers) using AI on Library or non-Library devices for Library work.
- Guidelines do NOT apply to personal use of AI on personal equipment.
- Violations may result in loss of access to AI systems.
- This document will be reviewed and updated periodically.
