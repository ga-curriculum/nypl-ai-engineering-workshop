# Exercise 2: Peer Testing Round-Robin

## Duration: 10 minutes

## Purpose
Refine your prompts by having someone else test them. A prompt that works for you might be ambiguous to someone else — peer testing reveals blind spots.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Exchange prompts (1 min)
- Pair up with the person next to you
- Share your high-context prompt from Exercise 1 (on screen, Slack, or paper)

### Step 2: Test your partner's prompt (4 min)
1. Paste their prompt into Gemini (don't modify it)
2. Evaluate the output:
   - Did it produce working Python code?
   - Was the output what you think your partner intended?
   - Were there any ambiguities that led to unexpected results?

### Step 3: Write feedback (3 min)
For your partner's prompt, note:

**Clarity:** Was the task obvious? (1-5)
**Completeness:** Were edge cases and types specified? (1-5)
**Output quality:** Did the AI produce usable code? (1-5)

### Step 4: Share and discuss (2 min)
- Share your feedback with your partner
- Discuss: What was the biggest difference between how you and your partner approached the same task?

## Reflection
- What assumptions did you make in your prompt that a peer didn't share?
- How would you change your prompt based on the feedback?
